About us

Two brothers, one shared household, and a lot of opinions about chore charts.

👋

Built by two brothers who split the chore chart growing up — and never quite agreed on whose turn it was.

At Home Software is our small, independent studio. No investors, no roadmap slideware — just two people who like building things for houses and the people who keep them running. We're starting with the problems we know best: our own kitchen table, our own shared calendar, our own "did the trash actually go out" arguments.

Everything we build starts the same way: something in our own house was annoying enough that we decided to fix it properly, then figured other households would want it too.
